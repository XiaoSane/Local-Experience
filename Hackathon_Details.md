## Local & Experiences — Intelligent Local Discovery & Experience Platform

### Problem Statement

<div align="justify" style="text-align: justify; text-justify: inter-word;">
When travelers visit a new destination, they often want to experience more than popular tourist attractions. They may be interested in local food, cultural experiences, festivals, workshops, adventure activities, hidden places, local events, shopping, nightlife, or experiences hosted by local communities. However, discovering these experiences is often difficult because information is spread across different websites, social media platforms, booking portals, review platforms, and local listings. The challenge becomes more complex because travelers have different interests, budgets, schedules, group sizes, accessibility requirements, and travel preferences. A traveler may have only three hours available, a family may be looking for child-friendly activities, or a visitor may want experiences within a particular budget and distance from their hotel. Generic search and recommendation platforms often do not consider all these factors together. At the same time, local restaurants, tour operators, activity providers, artists, event organizers, guides, and small businesses may struggle to reach travelers who are genuinely interested in their offerings. Many local experiences remain difficult to discover even when they are highly relevant to visitors.
</div>

### Hackathon Challenge

<div align="justify" style="text-align: justify; text-justify: inter-word;">
Design and prototype an intelligent local discovery and experience platform that connects travelers with relevant local experiences while helping local businesses and experience providers reach the right customers. The platform should create a personalized discovery experience where travelers can search or receive recommendations based on who they are, where they are, what they like, how much time they have, how much they want to spend, and what they are already planning to do. The solution should consider factors such as: Traveler interests and preferences, Current or planned location, Available time, Budget, Distance and travel time, Experience availability, Opening hours, Ratings and reviews, Group size and traveler type, Accessibility requirements and Existing itinerary. For example, instead of simply showing a list of restaurants or activities, the platform could understand that a family has two hours available near their hotel and wants an affordable local food and cultural experience, and recommend suitable options that can realistically fit within that time. The system should also be capable of adapting recommendations when circumstances change. For example, if an activity becomes unavailable, weather conditions change, the traveler has less time than expected, or their budget changes, the platform could suggest suitable alternatives. Teams may use AI, recommendation systems, location intelligence, personalization, data analytics, or conversational interfaces to improve discovery and planning. On the provider side, the platform may allow local businesses and experience providers to create listings, manage availability, define offerings, understand traveler preferences, and promote relevant experiences. The goal is to build a solution that moves beyond traditional search-and-list platforms and creates a more intelligent connection between travelers looking for meaningful local experiences and local businesses looking for relevant customers.
</div>
